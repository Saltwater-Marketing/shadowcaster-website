Our cold fronts are starting to move through with more consistency and that has been producing some great catches. Live Bait will remain the key to a successful day and it has been plentiful. As we move into December the channels and shallow rock piles will remain active with species like— Jacks, Snappers, Big Sharks, Muttons, Barracudas Etc. This coming week looks like we are in for some great weather before our next Cold Front rolls through. Its a great time of year to get out on the water for some nonstop light tackle action!!!!
