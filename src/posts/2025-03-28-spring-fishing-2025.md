Spring is our hands down favorite time to Fish in Key West. The **Tarpon** migration begins and the weather starts to warm up. April fishing in Key West kicks off our **Tarpon season** and the fish are showing up in the usual places. Fresh bait is the key and we are always on top of our bait game. We are offering a late afternoon/sunset Tarpon trip this year, which is prime feeding time for the **Silver King**.
