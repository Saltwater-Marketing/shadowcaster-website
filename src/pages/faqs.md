    intro: Here are answers to the most common questions about our trips. If you don’t see what you’re looking for, just give us a call at [305-587-7669](tel:+13055877669).
    items:
      - q: What Time Are The Trips?
        a: We generally run Morning and Afternoon trips. Trip time can vary slightly throughout the year, due to changes in conditions. In the Spring we run an evening Tarpon trip that departs at 5 PM for the amazing Evening Tarpon Bite.
      - q: What If The Weather Is Bad On Our Fishing Day?
        a: |-
          We are in the business of making our customers happy, so if we think the weather is not going to cooperate we’ll discuss our options with you. If we can reschedule we will, but if that’s not an option we will refund 100% of any money paid and we’ll try again next time.

          But Don’t worry—-The weather is always awesome in Key West!!!
      - q: Where Are You Located?
        a: |-
          A & B Marina 700 Front St

          We are conveniently located in the heart of Downtown Key West. Within walking distance of most major downtown hotels and guesthouses.
      - q: Can We Keep The Fish We Catch?
        a: Yes, however keeping different fish species may be seasonal – so ask your Captain which fish can be kept at the time of your charter. Your Captain will clean your fish and bag it up for you. We’ll even recommend restaurants that will cook your fresh catch for you!
      - q: Do We Need Fishing Licenses?
        a: Nope! Shadowcaster Charters provides all necessary licenses and permits for your days trip
      - q: Can We Bring Alcohol On A Fishing Charter?
        a: You Bet! We have large Yeti coolers full of ice and bottled waters, you can just throw your beers or other drinks right in.
      - q: How Many People Can Go On A Charter?
        a: Maximum is 4 anglers and all trips are private
      - q: How Long Are The Trips?
        a: We offer 4hr, 6hr and 8hr trips throughout the year. Our 4hr Sunset Tarpon Trips are offered March-June.
      - q: Will I Get Confirmation Of My Booking?
        a: Yes, every booking with Shadowcaster Charters gets a confirmation email detailing everything about your trip AND Capt John will contact you a day or 2 before your trip to go over any details needed.
      - q: What If We Would Like To Go Longer?
        a: Of course! If you’re on a charter with us and would like to extend your trip…we’ll be happy to accommodate as long as our booking calendar allows.
      - q: What Will We Catch?
        a: That is a tough one!!! It changes throughout the year–could be Tarpon, Snappers, Jacks, Sharks, Sea Trout, Barracudas just to name a few. But rest assured, our Captains at Shadowcaster are well prepared for the changing seasons and conditions to get it done.
      - q: Are We Allowed To Tip The Captain?
        a: Absolutely! Your Captain works extremely hard to provide guests with the best fishing experiences possible. Customarily, gratuities for a Captain are 20%+ of the cost of your charter.
