We are moving into our “cooler” months and that is going to bring some different species. While there are still some Tarpon to fish for (on the warmer days), we are mixing it up with some different types of fishing. This is the time of year that variety is key. We are going to target snappers, sharks, barracudas, jacks, sea trout, etc. So give us a call **305.587.7669**, drop us an email or book directly here on the site and we’ll get you out away from the crowds of Duval St for a few hours.
