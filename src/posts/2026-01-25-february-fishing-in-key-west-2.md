IT’S FREEZING ALL OVER THE REST OF THE COUNTRY—-NOT IN KEY WEST!!!!! As most are getting ready for a few days cooped up indoors, we are enjoying some cooler temps (60’s) here in Key West. The fishing around these cold fronts has been great. Awesome action remains on the shallow rocks and wrecks—snapper, jacks, cuda, cobia.  
Looking for some warmer weahter to start coming through in February. Tarpon Season is just right around the corner.
