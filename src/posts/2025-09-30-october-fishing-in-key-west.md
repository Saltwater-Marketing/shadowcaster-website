October Fishing in Key West is the official change of seasons for what we Target. This is the ‘Variety” Season. Meaning, we target everything from 100lb Tarpon to 3lb Snapper and everything in between. We target all these species with live biat, dead bait, artificial lures (hard and soft plastics). The Fishing in Key West changes hourly due to tides, temps, weather, wind, lighting, etc. And we always have the proper bait and tackle for the current situation. So, if you wanna get out Fishing the Backcountry of Key West give us a call anytime. **305.587.7669**
