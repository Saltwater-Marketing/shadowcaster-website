November fishing in Key West has been stellar. Our weather is about as perfect as you can get—light winds, clear skies, and high 70’s temps. Bait has been easier than past years making our days on the water fishing filled with action. The species we have been targeting, **Jacks, Barracudas, Snappers, sharks, Cobia,** etc have been eager to eat for us because of the cooling water temps. The forecast for the Holiday week is looking fantastic, and we still have a few spots open. Call Capt John Today **305.587.7669**
