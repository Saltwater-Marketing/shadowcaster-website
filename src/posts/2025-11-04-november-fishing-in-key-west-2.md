November Fishing in Key West has started off Strong!!! We love when the pelicans start to show up and dive on the bait schools—resulting in live wells full to the brim with Pilchards. Every fish in the ocean will eat a pilchard. We can take these guys to rock piles, channels, and shallow watewr wrecks for some non stop action. Snappers, Jacks, Barracudas, Cobia, Sharks all make up for a great day on the water.
