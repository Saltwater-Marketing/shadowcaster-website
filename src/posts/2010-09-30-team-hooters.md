Andrew Brace and his friend, Alan, came down from Tennessee for some great fishing this weekend on the ShadowCaster. Both Andrew and Alan are GM’s of the popular **Hooter’s** restaurant in Downtown Nashville. (and I thought I had a great job) We managed to dodge a few rain showers and put some fish in the boat. Over the two days of fishing both guys caught several nice **snook**, hooked four **Tarpon**and got two to the boat, a bunch of **Jacks** and **snappers**, 10+ **sharks** topping 125 pounds, a couple **bonefish** and one great big **Barracuda**. There is some Great fishing happening in the tourist “off season” so give me a call to take advantage of some great rates!!!!
