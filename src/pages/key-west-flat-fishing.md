      Light-Tackle & Sight-Fishing Across the Shallow Waters of Key West  
      The flats of Key West offer one of the most unique shallow-water fisheries in Florida. These clear, calm waters range from just a few inches to a few feet deep and create an ideal habitat for tarpon, barracuda, sharks, permit, jacks, snappers, and a wide variety of seasonal species.

      Fishing the flats is a visual, engaging experience. You can often see fish long before they reach the boat, watch how they react to bait or lures, and follow their movements across sand, turtle grass, and mangrove edges. For anglers looking to explore a quiet, scenic, and productive part of Key West, the flats are the perfect place to start.
