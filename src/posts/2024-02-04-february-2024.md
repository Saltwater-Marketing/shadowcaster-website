The Winter Species are here with the cooler water temps.  
The Pelicans have been bombing on bait along the shorelines and these baits are like candy to just about any fish that swims.  
Barracudas on the flats are on of my favorite fish to target. Explosive hits and lightening runs make for a great light tackle fight.  
Snappers have been gathering on the rock piles and patch reefs providing us with lots of action.  
And We’ve still been catching a few Tarpon on the warmer days.
