February Fishing in Key West has been great. We are seeing an abundance of **Cobia** this winter season. Cobia are a species that we will catch during the cooler months, but this year just seems to be over the top!!! Also, **Tarpon** are making thier way to Key West a little earlier than normal, with the really warm weather we have been having. Tarpon fishing should just continue to improve as we get into Spring and Early Summer. **Tarpon are probably the most prized inshore fish in the Florida Keys**.
