**October Fishing in Key West** remains to be great. If you are coming into town For **Fantasy Fest 2025**, don’t deny yourself the chance to get out on the water for some amazing Backcountry Fishing. The Tarpon Fishing in Key West remains to be an early morning thing. You gotta get out there early if you want the shot at catching the SilverKing. Pilchards remain to be the bait of choice to catch just about everything that swims. These are pics from the last couple days Fishing on **The ShadowCaster**. Fantasy Fest 2025 in Key West is going to be a very busy week here, so book your activities early and in advance. Call Capt John anytime to talk fishing **305.587.7669**
