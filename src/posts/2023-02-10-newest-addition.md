**The LilyPad** is our newest boat for 2023, so now in addition to world class fishing we also offer trips to the many sandbars and out islands only accessible by boat. With plenty of shade and comfortable seating,the **LilyPad** can accommodate up to 6 people. Sit back and relax on the cruise out, where you might see Dolphins, Sea Turtles and other wildlife.  
We’ve got it all covered:  
*Beach Chairs  
Shade Tent  
Noodles  
PaddleBoard  
Coolers with water  
Bluetooth Stereo  
And much more*
