There has been a great variety of species to target this March in Key West—-Cobias, Jacks, Snappers, Barracudas and of course the fan favorite—Tarpon!!!!

We are ramping up for out annual Tarpon Migration to begin—Fish are showing up more and more everyday. Our boats are ready-Tackles in tip top shape.

The calender is filling up, so give Capt John a call today to get out there for our World Class Key West Tarpon Fishing
