Early this week, here in Key West we experienced our first real cold front. The wind blew 20+ MPH and the temps dropped into the 60’s. That is now behind us and left us with perfect Winter time Key West fishing conditions. As the water temps drop it is going to start bringing in all those winter time species. Cobia, Jacks, Snappers, Barracudas and more are all on the menu now. Of course between cold fronts we still target our early morning resident Tarpon as well. The weather forecast looks amazing for the foreseeable future with light winds, clear skies, and warming temps.
