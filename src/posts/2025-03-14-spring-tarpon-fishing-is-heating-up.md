We are fast approaching the time of year we all wait for: **Tarpon Season**. Tarpon, in my opinion, are the most sought after fish the shallow waters of The Florida Keys and Key West has to offer. April fishing in Key West really kicks off Tarpon Season and fish are starting to show up already. A nice SouthEast breeze is one of the keys to success as it brings in the warm temps that Tarpon love. Tarpon tend to feed in the morning and late afternoons/early evening. We are offering Tarpon Sunset trips from 5PM-9PM. A great way to maximize oppurtunities, beat the heat of the day and catch the sunset from the water.
