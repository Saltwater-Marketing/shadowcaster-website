This is the time of year we have all been waiting for, **Tarpon Season 2026**. The weather starts to warm up, cold fronts become a thing of the past, water temps stabilize, and tide charts become our best friends—-That sets the stage for Key West World Class Tarpon Fishing.

We have many people return year after year to try their luck with the SilverKing—And the last 3 years have been some of the best Tarpon Fishing I can remember. It is going to be a few months of early mornings and long days—And we can’t wait!!!!

During the Spring Tarpon Migration (**March, April, May, June**) we offer an additional trip—Evening/Sunset Tarpon Trip. Take advantage of Tarpons preferred feeding time, beat the heat and catch a **Key West** Sunset out on the water. It’s really a great way to maximize your chances of hooking into The SilverKing.
